Structural Forces & Disruption
SECULAR themes persist 5-10+ years regardless of the business cycle. CYCLICAL themes are tied to the current macro environment and may reverse in 1-3 years.
AI & Compute Buildout ACCELERATINGSECULAR · 5-10Y+
The scale: Hyperscaler capex hitting $725B in 2026, up 77% YoY. Amazon ($200B), Microsoft ($190B), Alphabet ($175-185B), Meta ($115-135B). [CNBC]

What it means: Massive demand for GPUs (NVDA), custom silicon (TSM, MRVL), power infrastructure, and data center REITs. AI software monetization (MSFT Copilot, GOOGL AI Overviews) in early innings. Enterprise adoption still <10% penetrated.

The risk: ROI questions are growing. $725B needs to generate returns. If AI revenue disappoints in late 2026/2027, the capex cycle could decelerate — creating a semiconductor downcycle. [FactSet]

GLP-1 & Healthcare Transformation ACCELERATINGSECULAR · 10Y+
The scale: GLP-1 market projected at $82B in 2026, growing to $150B+ by 2030. ~20M Americans now on branded GLP-1s, reaching 30M by 2030. Oral formulations launching in 2026 massively expand access. [JPMorgan] [Morgan Stanley]

The disruption: JPMorgan estimates $30-55B in annual food & beverage revenue destruction by 2030. Users consume 21% fewer calories and spend 31% less on groceries. Snack food, fast food, alcohol, and bariatric surgery all losing demand. [CNBC]

Competition: Novo Nordisk, Lilly lead, but Amgen, Viking, Roche, AstraZeneca, Pfizer all have candidates. Pricing pressure inevitable.

Framework Summary
The cyclical regime signals caution, but structural forces add nuance. Two of the most powerful secular themes in decades — the $725B AI infrastructure buildout and the $80B+ GLP-1 revolution — are unfolding during a stagflationary macro environment. This creates conditions where sector-level dynamics may matter more than the broad market direction.

Sectors with dual tailwinds (favorable cyclical + structural positioning): Energy (inflation hedge + ME crisis), Healthcare (defensive + GLP-1), Utilities (defensive + AI power demand), and select Industrials (reshoring + aero aftermarket). See the Structural Forces tab for deeper analysis on each theme.

Tech presents a mixed signal. Cyclical indicators suggest vulnerability (elevated P/E, rising rates), but the AI capex cycle is historically unprecedented. Profitable AI infrastructure names (semis, cloud platforms) have structural support; speculative/unprofitable names carry more cyclical risk.

Key risk to monitor: Middle East escalation remains the highest-likelihood, highest-impact variable. A sustained oil spike above $120 would push the regime deeper into stagflation territory.
